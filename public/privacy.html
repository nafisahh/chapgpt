Roam privacy

Your journal, imported Timeline and personal photos are processed and stored in your browser. Exporting a backup includes this personal data.

Google place lookup sends only the selected place ID or coordinates through the local app server to Google. Public place photos and reviews load from Google. Google content is not stored in your journal backups; only the place ID is retained. The API key remains on the local server.

Optional Wikimedia searches send selected place coordinates to Wikimedia. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap and fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive network requests, including your IP address. No personal photos are uploaded by these features.

Google's handling of requests is described in the Google Privacy Policy. This local prototype has no accounts or online sharing.
